# Gallery photo updates

Put new or replacement photos in `originals/地域_施工事例名/`.
The two new-project folders are `originals/NEW-01_新規施工事例/` and
`originals/NEW-02_新規施工事例/`.

Website images are in `houses/施工事例ID/`, for example `houses/wakon-yosai-residence/`.
They are generated; keep original photos in `originals`.

Instructions: [IMAGE_UPDATES.md](../../docs/IMAGE_UPDATES.md).
Folder/page index: [project-folders.json](../../gallery/project-folders.json).

The website and originals are separate. Replacing a source photo does not publish
it until the update command, review and deployment have been completed.
